Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Short version: Mentaro does not collect, upload, sell or share your personal data. Your progress lives only on your own device.

What is stored, and where

Your training results, levels, streak, settings, optional name and optional age band are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They never leave your device unless you export them yourself.

What we do not do

We have no user accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party trackers. We do not run a server that receives your training data.

Third-party requests

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them. Google may log your IP address as part of that request. The app works without these fonts if the request is blocked.

Device sensors

Active Break can count steps with your phone's motion sensor, and only if you allow it. Motion data is processed live on your device and never stored or sent anywhere.

Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control all of it: Profile → Your data lets you export or delete everything, and clearing your browser's site data removes it too.

Children

Mentaro is intended for users aged 13 and over. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds optional online features such as leagues or cloud sync, this policy will be updated before that launches, and those features will be opt-in.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project's repository or contact the publisher listed on the website.
